Privacy Policy

Minuta is designed to be a completely private journaling experience. This policy reflects that commitment:

  1. **Data Collection & Storage:** Minuta does not collect any personally identifiable information. All journal entries, prompts, settings, and usage data are stored locally on your device only.
  2. **Data Transmission:** No journal entries or app data are transmitted to any external servers, including those operated by the developer.
  3. **Analytics:** Minuta does not use any third-party analytics to track user behavior within the app.

TLDR: **Your journal is 100% private and stays only on your device.**
