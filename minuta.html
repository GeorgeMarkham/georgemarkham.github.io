Minuta

The journalling app for those of us who hate journalling.

Minuta is the journalling app for those of us who hate journalling.

You have **140 characters only** to write your thoughts. Hit save and it'll appear in your journal for your eyes only!

Minuta will give you a prompt but it's up to you what you write!

Minuta is completely private. All of your journal entries stay on your device and only on your device.
